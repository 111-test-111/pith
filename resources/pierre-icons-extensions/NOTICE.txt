Pith file-type icon additions and artwork overrides
Copyright (c) 2026 Pith contributors

These additions extend the Pierre file-type icons from @pierre/trees
1.0.0-beta.4. Document silhouettes and the spreadsheet grid are adapted from
Pierre Computer Company's default/table icons; PDF, W, chart, music and play
marks and the revised spreadsheet header are Pith additions.
The squircle Sass override is an independently drawn Pith S monogram.
The unchanged Pierre palette supplies their light/dark colors.
